- quanta of electromagnetic radiation, from radio waves to gamma rays
- the [[bosons|boson]] that carries the electromagnetic force: spin 1, no mass, no charge
- energy $E = h\nu$ ($h$: Planck constant, $\nu$: frequency)
- travel at the speed of light, so the electromagnetic force has infinite range
- mass limit: < 10⁻¹⁸ eV/c²
- barely interact with each other (unlike [[gluons]])
- wave-particle duality: detected one at a time at single points, distributed like a wave (two-slit interference)
