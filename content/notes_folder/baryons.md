- [[hadrons]] made of three [[quarks]], bound by the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- half-integer spin → [[fermions]]
- lightest: [[protons|proton]] (uud) and [[neutrons|neutron]] (udd), the only long-lived ones
- hyperons (Λ, Σ, Ξ, Ω): contain strange quarks, decay in ~10⁻¹⁰ s
- baryon number +1 (antibaryons −1), never observed to change
- far more baryons than antibaryons in the universe, origin still unexplained
- neutron stars: held up by neutron degeneracy pressure and the short-range repulsion of the strong interaction, maximum mass ≈ 2.2–2.3 solar masses ([Fan et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.043052))
- baryonic matter ≈ 5% of the energy content of the universe (dark matter ≈ 27%, dark energy ≈ 68%)
