Energy available to do work at constant temperature (decreases in spontaneous processes, minimum at equilibrium).

Gibbs free energy (constant temperature and pressure):

$$
G = H - TS
$$

Helmholtz free energy (constant temperature and volume):

$$
A = U - TS
$$
where:
- $H$: enthalpy,
- $U$: internal energy,
- $T$: temperature (K),
- $S$: entropy.
