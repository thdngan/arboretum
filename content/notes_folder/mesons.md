- [[hadrons]] made of a [[quarks|quark]] and an antiquark, bound by the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- integer spin → [[bosons]]
- baryon number 0 → none are stable
- lightest: [[pions]]
- pion exchange: Yukawa's (1935) model of the nuclear force between [[protons]] and [[neutrons]], range ≈ 1.4 fm
- [[kaons]]: contain a strange quark, where CP violation was first seen
- charmonium (charm-anticharm) and bottomonium (bottom-antibottom): heavy pairs that behave like a hydrogen atom
