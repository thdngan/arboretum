---
title: slurry
tags:
  - notes
date: 2024-05-14
---
- fine solid particles suspended in a liquid (usually water), fluid enough to pump or pour
- too dilute → particles settle out, too concentrated → flow stops or pipes clog
- often non-Newtonian, with flow depending on shear rate and history (see [[rheology]])
- e.g. mud, ore slurries in pipelines, clathrate hydrate slurries (see [[clathrate]])
