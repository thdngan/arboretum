- atom or molecule with a net electric charge, from losing or gaining [[electrons]]
- cation (positive, e.g. Na⁺), anion (negative, e.g. Cl⁻), polyatomic ions (e.g. SO₄²⁻)
- carry electric current in solutions (salt water conducts, see [[dissociation]])
- plasma: gas ionised enough to be dominated by free electrons and ions (stars, ionosphere)
