- [[elementary particles|elementary]] particle, a charged [[leptons|lepton]] (first generation)
- charge $-e = -1.602\times10^{-19}$ C, spin 1/2 ([[fermions|fermion]])
- mass $9.109\times10^{-31}$ kg (0.511 MeV/c²), about 1/1836 of the [[protons|proton]] mass
- stable, no known internal structure
- antiparticle: [[positrons|positron]]
- in atoms: described by orbitals (wavefunctions), no classical path
