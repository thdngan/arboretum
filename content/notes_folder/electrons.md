An electron is a [[leptons|lepton]] of charge −1 and spin 1/2. It weighs 511 keV, about 1/1836 of a [[protons|proton]], and no experiment has found any internal structure in it.

Because it is charged it couples to the electromagnetic field, which is where chemistry and electronics come from.

It does not orbit the nucleus. It has a wavefunction, and the bound solutions of that wavefunction are the orbitals, each with a definite energy and shape but no trajectory anywhere in it. An electron can interfere with itself. It can also turn up on the far side of a barrier it hasn't got the energy to cross, which is tunnelling, and that sets the floor on how thin a transistor gate can get before current leaks straight through.
