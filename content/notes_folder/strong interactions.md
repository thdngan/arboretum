- strongest of the four fundamental interactions, range ~10⁻¹⁵ m (the size of a nucleus)
- acts on colour charge, carried by [[gluons]]
- binds [[quarks]] into [[hadrons]] (e.g. [[protons]], [[neutrons]])
- confinement: force between quarks stays roughly constant with distance → no isolated quarks
- asymptotic freedom: weak at very short distances (Nobel Prize 2004)
- theory: quantum chromodynamics (QCD)
- residual strong (nuclear) force: binds protons and neutrons into nuclei, reaches only nearest neighbours
- no stable nuclei heavier than lead: electric repulsion across the whole nucleus wins
