- breaking of a molecule or compound into smaller parts ([[ions]], atoms, radicals, smaller molecules), often reversible
- in water: salts, acids and bases → ions (e.g. NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻), acid strength given by the dissociation constant $K_a$
- thermal dissociation: by heat
- photodissociation (photolysis): by an absorbed [[photons|photon]], e.g. O₂ → 2 O for λ < 242 nm (first step of ozone formation)
- [[clathrate]] hydrates: break down into gas and water (or ice) outside their stability field
- dissociation pressure of a clathrate: minimum gas pressure at which it is stable, at a given temperature
