- particles with integer spin (0, 1, 2, ...), vs. [[fermions]] (half-integer)
- no Pauli exclusion: any number can share one quantum state (lasers, Bose-Einstein condensates)
- force carriers (spin 1): [[photons|photon]] (electromagnetism), [[gluons]] (strong), W⁺, W⁻ and Z⁰ ([[weak interactions|weak]])
- Higgs boson (spin 0): excitation of the Higgs field, which gives mass to the W and Z bosons, [[quarks]] and charged [[leptons]]
- hypothetical: [[gravitons|graviton]] (spin 2), [[axions]], [[majorons]]
- composite bosons contain an even number of fermions, e.g. [[mesons]], helium-4 atoms (superfluid below 2.2 K)
