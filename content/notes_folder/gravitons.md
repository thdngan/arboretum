- hypothetical carrier of [[gravity]], if gravity is quantised
- massless (infinite range), spin 2 (couples to energy and momentum, always attractive)
- never observed
- theory breaks down near the Planck energy (~10¹⁹ GeV) → the problem of quantum gravity
- single-graviton detection: long thought impossible (Dyson), recent resonator proposals ([Tobar et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51420-8)), though a classical wave could give the same signal ([Carney et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.044009))
