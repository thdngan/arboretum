- one of the four fundamental interactions, the only one that changes [[quarks|quark]] or [[leptons|lepton]] flavour (e.g. d → u)
- carriers: W⁺ and W⁻ (80.4 GeV/c²) and Z⁰ (91.2 GeV/c²) [[bosons]]
- very short range (~10⁻¹⁸ m), because the carriers are heavy
- β decay: inside a [[neutrons|neutron]], d → u + e⁻ + ν̄ₑ
- first step of the Sun's fusion: p + p → ²H + e⁺ + νₑ
- violates parity (W bosons couple only to left-handed particles and right-handed antiparticles) and CP (first seen in [[kaons|kaon]] decays, 1964)
- unified with electromagnetism in the electroweak theory
- together with gravity, the only interactions [[neutrinos]] feel
