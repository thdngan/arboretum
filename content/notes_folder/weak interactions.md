The weak interaction is the only force that turns one kind of [[quarks|quark]] or [[leptons|lepton]] into another. Everything else leaves flavour alone. A [[protons|proton]] can only become a [[neutrons|neutron]] weakly, which is why beta decay exists and why the Sun burns at all, since the first step of hydrogen fusion is two protons meeting and one of them converting.

The carriers are the W± and Z⁰, at 80.4 and 91.2 GeV. Massive carriers give a force of finite range, about 10⁻¹⁸ m, and the weakness is mostly that: at everyday energies the interaction is suppressed by the W mass rather than by a small coupling. Above 100 GeV or so the weak and electromagnetic couplings are comparable, which is what electroweak unification amounts to.

It also violates parity, and not by a little. The W couples only to left-handed particles and right-handed antiparticles, so the mirror image of a weak process is not a process that occurs at all. It is the one interaction that tells left from right, and through the complex phase in the CKM matrix, the only one that tells matter from antimatter.
