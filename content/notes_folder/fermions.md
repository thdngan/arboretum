A particle that has a half-odd-integer spin (spin 1/2, spin 3/2, etc.)

Fermions include:
- [[quarks]]
- [[leptons]]
- some [[composite particles]], like [[baryons]] ([[protons]], [[neutrons]])

Quarks and leptons (like [[electrons]]) are elementary fermions, and together they make up all ordinary matter.
