Total power emitted per unit area by a [[blackbody]]:

$$
F = \sigma T^4
$$
where:
- $F$: emitted flux (W m⁻²),
- $\sigma$: Stefan-Boltzmann constant, $5.67\times10^{-8}$ W m⁻² K⁻⁴,
- $T$: temperature (K).

For a real (grey) body with emissivity $0 \le \varepsilon \le 1$:

$$
F = \varepsilon\sigma T^4
$$
