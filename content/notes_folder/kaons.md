- lightest [[mesons]] containing a strange [[quarks|quark]] (strange quark or antiquark, with an up or down one)
- decay only via the [[weak interactions|weak interaction]] → long-lived (charged kaons ≈ 12 ns)
- neutral kaons oscillate between kaon and antikaon → a short-lived state (≈ 0.09 ns) and a long-lived one (≈ 51 ns)
- CP violation first seen here: long-lived neutral kaon → two [[pions]] (Cronin and Fitch, 1964)
