Neutrinos are neutral [[leptons]]. No electric charge and no colour means they only interact through the [[weak interactions|weak force]] and gravity, and the weak cross-section is small enough that a solar neutrino crossing the whole Earth has something like a one in 10¹¹ chance of touching anything. About 6×10¹⁰ of them pass through every square centimetre of you every second.

There are 3 flavours, attached to the [[electrons|electron]], the [[muons|muon]] and the [[tauons|tauon]]. A neutrino produced as one flavour can be detected as another, because the flavour states are mixtures of 3 states of definite mass and those propagate at slightly different rates. Oscillation only works if at least 2 of the masses are nonzero, so this settled a question the Standard Model had answered the other way (Standard Model assumed neutrinos were massless).

The masses themselves are still unmeasured. Oscillation gives the differences between squared masses and not the values, direct limits put the electron-neutrino mass below about 0.5 eV, cosmology puts the sum of all three below roughly 0.1 eV, and even the ordering of the three is unknown. Whether a neutrino is its own antiparticle is also open, and neutrinoless double beta decay is the experiment that would decide it.
