- third-generation charged [[leptons|lepton]]: 1.777 GeV/c², ~3500× the [[electrons|electron]] mass (~1.9 [[protons|proton]] masses)
- lifetime ≈ 2.9 × 10⁻¹³ s
- the only lepton heavy enough to decay into [[hadrons]] (~65% of decays), useful for studying the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- tau neutrino: first directly detected in 2000 (DONUT, Fermilab)
