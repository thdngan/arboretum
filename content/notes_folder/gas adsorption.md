- gas molecules sticking to a surface (adsorption), vs. absorption: uptake into the bulk
- scales with surface area → porous adsorbents (activated carbon: ~10³ m² per gram)
- physisorption: weak van der Waals forces, easily reversed
- chemisorption: chemical bond with the surface (~10× stronger), selective, often irreversible
- adsorption isotherm: amount adsorbed vs. pressure, at fixed temperature
- Langmuir isotherm (identical independent sites, one molecule each): $\theta = \frac{KP}{1+KP}$, with $\theta$ the fraction of sites occupied, $P$ the gas pressure, $K$ an equilibrium constant
- guest molecules filling [[clathrate]] cages: Langmuir-type adsorption, in 3D
