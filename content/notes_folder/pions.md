- lightest [[mesons]], made of up and down [[quarks]] and antiquarks: π⁺, π⁻, π⁰
- mass ≈ 140 MeV/c² (charged) and 135 MeV/c² (neutral), ~15% of a [[protons|proton]]'s
- unusually light: pseudo-Goldstone bosons of spontaneously broken chiral symmetry
- π⁺ and π⁻: lifetime ≈ 26 ns, decay into a [[muons|muon]] and a [[neutrinos|neutrino]] (source of most cosmic-ray muons at the ground)
- π⁰: lifetime ≈ 8.5 × 10⁻¹⁷ s, decays into two [[photons]]
- pion exchange describes the nuclear force between [[protons]] and [[neutrons]] (Yukawa, 1935)
