Gravity is the weakest of the four fundamental forces by an enormous margin, and also the one that shapes the largest structures in the cosmos. Between two protons it is around 10³⁶ times weaker than the electrostatic repulsion. It wins at large scales anyway, because it only ever attracts and there is no gravitational charge to cancel against, while electric charges do cancel and mostly have.

The other forces are described by quantum field theory. Gravity is described by Einstein’s General Theory of Relativity, which says something different in kind: mass and energy curve spacetime, and an object with no force acting on it follows the straightest path available through the curved version. Essentially why the Sun keeps planets in orbit, why black holes can trap even light, and why time (man what even is time?) itself runs slower in strong gravitational fields, which GPS satellites have to correct for at about 38 microseconds a day.

In the quantum world, gravity remains a mystery. Every other fundamental force has a corresponding particle: the [[photons|photon]] for electromagnetism, [[gluons]] for the [[strong interactions|strong force]], and W/Z [[bosons]] for the [[weak interactions|weak force]]. But gravity’s hypothetical particle, the [[gravitons|graviton]], has never been detected, probably cannot be, and the theory built around it stops predicting anything above the Planck energy.

Gravitational waves are the one thing that did turn up. Ripples in spacetime from accelerating masses, predicted in 1916 and finally detected in 2015 from two black holes of around 30 solar masses merging. The strain at Earth was about 10⁻²¹, a length change of roughly 4×10⁻¹⁸ m across LIGO’s 4 km arms, which is about a two-hundredth of the radius of a [[protons|proton]]. It's fascinating that even though we live with gravity every moment of our lives, we still don’t fully understand it. Whether it has a quantum nature, whether there's a deeper theory underneath Einstein’s equations, whether extra dimensions explain why it's so weak, all of that is still open.
