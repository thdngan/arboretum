- one of the four fundamental interactions, by far the weakest (between two protons, ~10⁻³⁶ of the electric force)
- always attractive, infinite range → dominates at large scales
- Newton: $F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}$, with $G = 6.674\times10^{-11}$ N m² kg⁻²
- general relativity: mass and energy curve spacetime, free bodies follow the straightest paths (geodesics)
- clocks run slower deeper in a gravity well (man what even is time?)
- GPS: satellite clocks gain ≈ 45 μs/day from weaker gravity and lose ≈ 7 μs/day from orbital speed (net ≈ +38 μs/day)
- hypothetical carrier: [[gravitons|graviton]] (never detected), no complete quantum theory of gravity yet
- gravitational waves: first detected by LIGO in 2015 (two merging black holes of ~30 solar masses)
