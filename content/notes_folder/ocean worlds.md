- bodies with a large liquid-water ocean, usually under an ice shell
- mostly icy moons of the outer solar system (Earth also counts)
- strong cases: Europa (Jupiter), Enceladus and Titan (Saturn)
- Mimas (Saturn): young ocean inferred from its orbital motion ([Lainey et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06975-9))
- likely or possible: Ganymede, Callisto, Triton, Pluto
- evidence mostly indirect: gravity, induced magnetic fields, rotation, plumes
- Enceladus plumes: ocean material sampled by Cassini, including phosphates ([Postberg et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-05987-9))
- salty water, possibly with ammonia (antifreeze)
