A proton is a [[baryons|baryon]] of charge +1, made of two up [[quarks]] and one down, confined by the [[strong interactions|strong force]]. The number of them in a nucleus fixes the element.

No proton decay has ever been seen. Super-Kamiokande puts the lifetime above 10³⁴ years for the channels it can detect. Most grand unified theories want the proton to decay, so the null result constrains those theories more than it says anything about protons.

uud is bookkeeping for quantum numbers rather than a description of the interior. The three valence quarks account for about 1% of the mass, and the remainder is energy stored in the [[gluons|gluon]] field along with the quark-antiquark pairs it keeps generating. The charge radius comes out at 0.841 fm, though for about a decade measurements on muonic hydrogen and on ordinary hydrogen disagreed about it by some 4%, which resolved in favour of the smaller value around 2019.
