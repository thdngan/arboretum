- [[composite particles|composite]] particle, a [[baryons|baryon]]: two up [[quarks]] and one down quark (uud), bound by the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- charge $+e$, spin 1/2
- mass $1.673\times10^{-27}$ kg (938.3 MeV/c²)
- number of protons in a nucleus = atomic number (which element it is)
- radius ≈ 0.84 fm (the "proton radius puzzle" now looks settled on this value, [Maisenbacher et al., 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10124-3))
- no decay observed: lifetime > $2.4\times10^{34}$ yr for p → e⁺ + π⁰ ([Takenaka et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.112011))
- the three quarks' own masses are ~1% of the proton mass, the rest is quark motion and [[gluons|gluon]] field energy
