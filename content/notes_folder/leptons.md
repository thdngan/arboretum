A lepton is an elementary particle with spin 1/2 that does not interact strongly ([[strong interactions|strong interactions]])

Two main types of leptons:
- charged leptons: [[electrons|electrons]] (first generation), [[muons|muons]] (second generation), [[tauons|tauons]] (third generation)
- neutral leptons: [[neutrinos|neutrinos]]
