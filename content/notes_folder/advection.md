title: advection
date: 2022-08-01
---
Transport of a quantity by the bulk motion of a fluid (e.g. [[heat]] or moisture carried by wind):

$$
\frac{\partial c}{\partial t} + \mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla c = 0
$$
where:
- $c$: transported quantity (concentration, temperature, ...),
- $\mathbf{u}$: flow velocity,
- $\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla c$: advection term.

Relative importance of advection and [[diffusion]] (Péclet number), with $U$, $L$ typical velocity and length scales and $D$ the diffusivity:

$$
Pe = \frac{UL}{D}
$$
