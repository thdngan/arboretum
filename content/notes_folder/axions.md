- hypothetical, very light, weakly interacting [[bosons|boson]]
- proposed in the late 1970s (Peccei and Quinn, Weinberg, Wilczek) to solve the strong CP problem: why the CP-violating parameter of QCD is below ~10⁻¹⁰ (from the [[neutrons|neutron]] electric dipole moment limit)
- dark matter candidate
- search: axion → [[photons|photon]] conversion in a strong magnetic field (e.g. ADMX microwave cavity, scanning one mass step at a time)
