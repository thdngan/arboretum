- [[elementary particles|elementary]] [[fermions]] (spin 1/2) that make up [[hadrons]], e.g. [[protons]] (uud) and [[neutrons]] (udd)
- 6 flavours in 3 generations: up/down, charm/strange, top/bottom
- electric charge +2/3 (u, c, t) or −1/3 (d, s, b)
- colour charge (red, green, blue): the charge of the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- the only elementary particles that feel all four fundamental interactions
- confinement: never seen alone, only in colour-neutral combinations ([[baryons]]: three quarks, [[mesons]]: quark + antiquark)
- asymptotic freedom: nearly free at very short distances
- heavier flavours decay into lighter ones via the [[weak interactions|weak interaction]]
- masses from ~2 MeV/c² (up) to ~173 GeV/c² (top)
- top quark: decays in ~5 × 10⁻²⁵ s, before it can form hadrons (toponium reported at the LHC in 2025: [CMS Collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22382), [ATLAS Collaboration](https://atlas.cern/Updates/Press-Statement/Quasi-Bound-Tops))
