Quarks are spin-1/2 [[fermions]] with fractional electric charge, +2/3 for up, charm and top and −1/3 for down, strange and bottom. They are the only particles that feel all 4 forces.

They also carry colour charge, which has nothing to do with colour and is just the name for the three-valued charge of the [[strong interactions|strong force]]. Colour is why no quark has ever been seen alone. Pull two apart and the energy in the field between them keeps growing with distance, until making a new quark-antiquark pair costs less than stretching further, so what comes out of a collision is [[hadrons]] and never a bare quark (confinement).

At short distances the coupling runs the other way. Quarks sitting close together inside a proton behave almost as free particles, which is asymptotic freedom, and it is the reason perturbative QCD works at high energy at all.

The 6 flavours group into 3 generations by mass:

- up (2.3 MeV) and down (4.8 MeV), which build [[protons]] and [[neutrons]]
- strange (95 MeV) and charm (1.28 GeV)
- bottom (4.18 GeV) and top (173 GeV)

Everything past the first generation decays. The top is its own case: it goes in about 5×10⁻²⁵ s, which is shorter than hadronization takes, so it is the one quark that never ends up inside a hadron.
