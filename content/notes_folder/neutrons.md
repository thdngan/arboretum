- [[composite particles|composite]] particle, a [[baryons|baryon]]: one up [[quarks|quark]] and two down quarks (udd)
- no electric charge, spin 1/2
- mass $1.675\times10^{-27}$ kg (939.6 MeV/c²), about 0.14% heavier than the [[protons|proton]]
- stable inside stable nuclei, free neutrons β-decay: n → p + e⁻ + ν̄ₑ
- free lifetime ≈ 880 s (~15 min), with bottle (≈ 878 s) and beam (≈ 888 s) measurements still disagreeing ([CERN Courier, 2025](https://cerncourier.com/a/the-beam-bottle-debate-at-psi/))
- heavy nuclei have more neutrons than protons (²⁰⁸Pb: 82 p, 126 n), since neutrons add strong attraction without electric repulsion
