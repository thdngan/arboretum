- fraction of incident radiation reflected by a surface or body
- 0 (absorbs everything) to 1 (reflects everything)
- Bond albedo: over all wavelengths and directions (the one used in energy balance)
- geometric albedo: brightness at zero phase angle, relative to a flat, perfectly diffusing disk
- Bond albedos: Earth ≈ 0.30, Venus ≈ 0.76, Moon ≈ 0.11
- fresh snow ≈ 0.8, open ocean ≈ 0.06
- ice-albedo feedback: less ice → darker surface → more sunlight absorbed → more melting (positive feedback)
- role in a planet's temperature: [[Stefan-Boltzmann law]], [[naked planet climate model]]
