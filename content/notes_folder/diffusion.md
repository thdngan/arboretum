Net spreading from high to low concentration through random molecular motion.

Flux (Fick's first law):

$$
J = -D\,\frac{\partial c}{\partial x}
$$

Diffusion equation (Fick's second law):

$$
\frac{\partial c}{\partial t} = D\,\frac{\partial^2 c}{\partial x^2}
$$
where:
- $J$: diffusive flux,
- $c$: concentration,
- $D$: diffusion coefficient (m² s⁻¹),
- $x$, $t$: position and time.

Spreading distance after a time $t$: $\sim\sqrt{Dt}$. Turbulent mixing is often modelled with the same equation and a much larger effective $D$.
