Vapour pressure vs. temperature, when one of the two phases is a gas:

$$
\frac{d\ln P}{dT}=\frac{\Delta H_{\text{vap}}}{RT^2}
$$
where:
- $P$: saturation vapour pressure,
- $T$: temperature (K),
- $\Delta H_{\text{vap}}$: molar enthalpy of vaporisation (of sublimation, for solid to gas),
- $R$: universal gas constant.

General form for any two phases (Clapeyron equation), with $\Delta V$ the molar volume change:

$$
\frac{dP}{dT}=\frac{\Delta H}{T\,\Delta V}
$$
