- [[bosons]] (spin 1, massless) that carry the [[strong interactions|strong interaction]]
- hold [[quarks]] together inside [[hadrons]]
- carry colour charge themselves (a colour and an anticolour): 8 kinds
- interact with each other, unlike [[photons]] → the field between quarks forms a narrow tube → confinement
