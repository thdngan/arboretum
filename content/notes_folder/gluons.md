Gluons are the gauge [[bosons]] of the [[strong interactions|strong force]], massless and spin 1. There are 8 of them, matching the 8 generators of SU(3).

An important property is that a gluon carries colour charge itself, in some combination of a colour and an anticolour. A [[photons|photon]] mediates electromagnetism without carrying electric charge, so photons ignore each other. Gluons do not ignore each other, and the field between 2 [[quarks]] gets pulled into a narrow tube instead of spreading out. The energy in that tube grows linearly with separation, which is where colour confinement comes from.
