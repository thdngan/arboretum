Idealised perfect absorber (and emitter) of radiation, whose emission depends only on its temperature.

Spectral radiance (Planck's law):

$$
B_\lambda(T) = \frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5}\,\frac{1}{e^{hc/\lambda k_B T}-1}
$$
where:
- $B_\lambda$: spectral radiance (W m⁻² sr⁻¹ m⁻¹),
- $\lambda$: wavelength,
- $T$: temperature (K),
- $h$: Planck constant,
- $c$: speed of light,
- $k_B$: Boltzmann constant.

Peak wavelength (Wien's displacement law), with $b \approx 2.898\times10^{-3}$ m K:

$$
\lambda_{\max} = \frac{b}{T}
$$

Integrated over all wavelengths and directions: [[Stefan-Boltzmann law]].
