- hypothetical massless (or very light) [[bosons|boson]] from spontaneously broken lepton number
- linked to Majorana [[neutrinos]] (neutrino = its own antiparticle) and seesaw models of small neutrino masses
- would interact almost only with neutrinos
- search: neutrinoless double beta decay with majoron emission → continuous spectrum for the summed energy of the two [[electrons]] (instead of a single peak)
